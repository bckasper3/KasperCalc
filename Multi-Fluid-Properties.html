Comparing Properties Across Common Fluids

This page collects specific gravity, kinematic viscosity, and dynamic (absolute) viscosity vs. temperature for a broad cross-section of fluids used across aerospace, industrial, and automotive applications including military hydraulic and lubricating oils, phosphate-ester and silicone fluids, glycols, fuels, and common reference liquids. The charts and calculators below cover the fluids listed by category in the table below.
